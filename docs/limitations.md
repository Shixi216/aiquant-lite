# Limitations

- Point-in-time fundamental coverage is insufficient for formal full-market 60/40 evaluation.
- Sentiment, policy/news, capital flow, and the composite remain shadow-only.
- The market scanner is a research-priority tool, not a recommendation engine.
- Snapshot freshness depends on the controlled local data process.
- Historical replay uses RAW prices and has survivorship, corporate-action, benchmark, feature,
  and limited-window risks.
- WeCom is a local adapter in Stage 11; production delivery is not deployed or proven.
- Manual trade records describe external user actions; they do not connect to a broker.
- No broker, QMT, xtquant, real-order, or automatic execution capability exists.
- Backtest and experiment results do not prove stable profitability.
- Stage 11 does not include a desktop UI, installer packaging, or Stage 12 deliverables.
